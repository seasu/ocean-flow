# AUDIO PROFILE: 阿公 (Taiwanese grandfather storyteller)
A man in his mid-seventies from Taiwan. Weathered, slightly raspy, breathy voice with the texture of age; warm but carrying a lifetime of stories.

### DIRECTOR'S NOTES
Accent: Taiwanese Mandarin (台灣國語), not mainland Putonghua. Soft retroflexes, Taiwan intonation.
Style: an old man telling an ancient legend to the young, calm and knowing.
Pacing: fluent, steady storytelling pace like a normal conversation, about 5 Chinese characters per second. Keep pauses at commas very short. Do not drag or linger. Do not artificially deepen the voice.
