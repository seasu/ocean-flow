## Timing notes

- Shot 04 is the only tight one (28 words in 8 s). Plan: start it right after "Until one day…" at about 0:15, using the spare time in Shot 03, which gives it about 11 s.
- 「機緣」 is rendered as "destiny" in both Shot 10 and the tagline so the closing line echoes it. Alternative tagline if "destiny" feels too grand: "Step out, and find your fortune."
- 「道」 is "path" in Shot 06 (brand line) and "the Way" in Shot 07, where it sits next to qi and veins as a named concept.
